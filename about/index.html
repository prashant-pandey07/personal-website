About Prashant Pandey

Programmer, researcher, builder, experimenter, and writer of technical ideas. This is my story — what I build, how I think, and where I'm headed.

Who I Am

I'm a student and self-taught programmer with a focus on building real, usable software. I research ideas that I find genuinely interesting, and I document everything openly so others can learn from it too.

I grew up taking things apart to understand how they work — and I've never stopped. That mindset drives everything I do: from reverse-engineering a problem to designing a system from scratch, I believe deeply in understanding the fundamentals before reaching for abstraction.

I'm not a casual hobbyist. I treat every project like a serious engineering exercise, with real requirements, thoughtful architecture, and documented outcomes. I care about building things that actually work.

How I Think About Technology

I believe most technology problems are solvable with careful thinking, not just more code. My approach is:

  • Understand the problem before writing a line. Many projects fail because the problem was never properly defined.
  • Prefer simplicity. Complex solutions are harder to debug, maintain, and explain. The best code is usually the least code.
  • Build offline-first. Systems that depend entirely on connectivity are fragile. I design for resilience.
  • Document as you go. If you can't explain what you built clearly, you don't fully understand it yet.
  • Iterate publicly. Sharing work-in-progress invites better feedback than waiting for perfection.

Long-Term Mission

"I want to build systems that are genuinely useful, share the knowledge behind them openly, and push the boundary of what a single determined programmer can accomplish."

This website is the public record of that mission. Every project, research article, and idea published here is a step toward building something that matters — and doing it transparently so others can learn from the process.
